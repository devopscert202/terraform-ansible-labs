Never used Terraform before? Read these two pages first, in this order

Both primers assume you know nothing. The first explains the tool, the second explains the cloud it will be talking to. Together they take about forty minutes, and they are the difference between following lab00 and understanding it.

1

Terraform 101 →

What Terraform is and who owns it, what HCL is and the anatomy of a block, what a provider is and how to add one, every version-constraint operator including ~>, the CLI commands and the plan symbols, what state is, and what drift means.

Answers: what am I actually running?

AWS Primer →

Region and availability zone, VPC and CIDR, public and private subnets, internet gateway, route table, security group, EC2 instance, key pair, and IAM access keys — each with its Terraform resource type, plus the diagram of what Lab 10 builds.

Answers: what is a VPC?

Lab 00 →

Now start typing. Verify your Terraform and AWS CLI versions, export your access keys, write your first provider block, and run terraform init. Creates nothing billable.

WhereWhat it is for

docs/Longer written deep dives, one flat numbered file per subject, for after the lab.
